Research

My work spans mechanistic interpretability, computational reasoning, and model merging. I’m interested in how models can keep learning from interaction without forgetting what they already know, and in methods for transferring capabilities between models without access to their original training data. I also want to move interpretability beyond experiments designed by hand, toward agents that can construct, run, and verify them.
